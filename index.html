      One size fits all

      Questions are conditional, not fixed

      Choose a single day out and the multi-day questions are struck out on the right, with a count. The same engine serves both, so there is one form to maintain rather than one per tour type.

      Blank fields

      The form answers back

      Anywhere marked in ochre, a customer can ask about the question instead of guessing or skipping. The reply can also fill the field in for them.

      Unbookable answers

      Answers are checked before they arrive

      Room allocation is counted against group size. The old form asked for single, double and twin in one text box; a test response answered it with the number two.

      Handover

      Structured output, with a completeness score

      Sending produces a record ready for the pipeline sheet plus a note on what is still unknown, so it is clear at a glance whether an enquiry can be quoted or needs a reply first.
